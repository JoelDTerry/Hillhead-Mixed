Game 1 — Downfield Cutting Patterns

I want us to work on our cutting patterns more and really ingrain them into our game. In addition to this, I also want us to focus on our movement when we are meant to be the inactive cutter. Too often we want to get involved, leading to swamping the disc, bringing in defenders and cutting off much better cuts which the active cutters are making. Be disciplined — don't get involved when it is not your turn. Instead, anticipate the next pass and set yourself up for it.

Focus
  • Open side slash to the breakside
  • Cut deep from the breakside
  • If in doubt, stay still — fight the urge to cut into open space when it's not your turn
Game Format
Format Game to 5, or 30 minutes
Rule Slow start every time
Rule Turnover if the first cuts are not the default cutting patterns
Rule Whistle blown every time someone cuts when they should hold — play on though
