10 May

Communication & Confidence — 3 hours, 2:00 PM

Session Aims
  • Practise the basics
  • Work on our communication skills
  • Take on options and grow confidence
Session Rule In all games, FMPs must pick up the disc after a turnover. This can be for a quick start or slow start.
2:00

Session Start — Warm Up

2:15

Break Force Drill

5 minutes inside force · 5 minutes flat force

2:30

Game 1 — No Talking

Here I want you to work on your body language and your field awareness. Use your eyes to see what's happening on the field.

Game Format
Format 20 minute game duration
Format Max 120 seconds between points · Agree ABBA or fixed ratio for game starts
Rule 30 seconds between points (starts as soon as a team scores)
Rule The only talking permitted: stall count, and when a foul is called
Rule All other communication must benonverbal
2:50

Game 2 — One Speaker Per Team

Time to step up and lead. If you can speak, make sure to use your voice to maximise your team's chances.

Game Format
Format 20 minute game duration
Format Max 120 seconds between points · Agree ABBA or fixed ratio for game starts
Rule One person per team may talk each point — chosen by the captains at the start of each point
Rule The same person cannot speak twice in a row · Must alternate between genders
Rule The only other talking permitted: stall count, and when a foul is called
Rule All other communication must be nonverbal
⏸   3:10 – 3:15   Break
3:15

Red Light, Green Light

Green light - Take on Options, push yourself to do more. Red light - Dial it back, don't take on risks.

Game Format
Format 20 minutes per Gender
Format A Jail is placed on the sideline at halfway. If you turn over when on Red light, you must go to jail for 10 seconds, while the game continues.
Jail Turnover on a progressive option (resets and swings don't count) → thrower goes to jail for 10 seconds while play continues
Jail Timekeeper may also send you to jail for excessive risk (e.g. contested huck, poaching on D)

🔴 Red Light

  • Take conservative options
  • Play safe — reset early
  • Don't rush decisions or execution
  • Don't hog the disc
  • On D: play tight, no poaching, no risks

Play continues while a player travels to and from jail — don't walk or your team suffers longer.

🟢 Green Light

  • Take progressive options
  • Look for break throws or hucks
  • Don't rush to reset — stay in active lanes
  • On D: take risks, look to poach into the lane
⏸   3:55 – 4:00   Break
4:00

Kung Fu Throwing

Keep all discs below head height. Play with your pivot position and release point. Bring intensity and focus.

Throwing Sequence

10 yards apart

  1. Flat backhands — 30 seconds
  2. Flat forehands — 30 seconds
  3. Fake backhand, pivot over, throw forehand — 30 seconds
  4. Fake forehand, pivot over, throw backhand — 30 seconds
  5. Soft, delicate, touchy forehands — 30 seconds
  6. Half-pivot backhands — 30 seconds

20 yards apart

  1. Flat backhands — 45 seconds
  2. Flat forehands — 45 seconds
  3. Fake backhand, pivot over, throw forehand — 45 seconds
  4. Fake forehand, pivot over, throw backhand — 45 seconds
  5. Hard, fast, controlled backhands — 45 seconds
  6. Hard, fast, controlled forehands — 45 seconds

2 yards apart

  1. Throw how you catch — 40 catches. Challenge the catcher: make some high, some low, some fast, some weird spin.
