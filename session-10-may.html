10 May

Communication & Confidence — 3 hours, 2:00 PM

Session Aims
  • Practise the basics
  • Work on our communication skills
  • Take on options and grow confidence
Session Rule

In all games, FMPs must pick up the disc after a turnover. This can be for a quick start or slow start.

2:00

Session Start — Warm Up

2:15

4 Lines / Buzz Bullets

  • Uplines — outside and flat forces
  • Out and under — outside and away force
  • Out — outside force; thrower starts with a small pop pass

5 minutes on each force (25 minutes total)

2:40

Single Gender Drills

Don't faff — get straight into these drills. 10 minutes on each drill.

FAT Unders

5 minutes backhand, 5 minutes forehand

  • Throw hard and low
  • Attack the disc when catching
  • Cut hard

Break Force

  • Throw early
  • Throw with touch and control
  • Use body language to sell your fakes
3:05
⏸   3:05 – 3:10   Break
3:10

Game 1 — No Talking

Game Format
Format

Game duration is 20 minutes. Max 120 seconds between points. Agree on ABBA or fixed ratio between game starts.

Rule

The only talking allowed is the stall count and when a foul is called. 30 seconds between points (starts as soon as a team scores).

Rule

All other communication must be nonverbal.

3:30

Game 2 — One Speaker Per Team

Game Format
Format

Game duration is 20 minutes. Max 120 seconds between points. Agree on ABBA or fixed ratio between game starts.

Rule

One person is allowed to talk per team, per point. Each point, captains pick a new speaker — can't be the same person twice, and must alternate between genders.

Rule

Outside of the speaker, only the stall count and foul calls are permitted. Everything else must be nonverbal.

3:50
⏸   3:50 – 4:00   Break
4:00

Red Light, Green Light

20 minutes per gender. 4:00 – 4:40.

Game Format — Red Light / Green Light
Format

20 minutes per gender group.

Rule

Jail — positioned on the sideline at halfway. Play continues while a player travels to and from jail. Don't walk, or your team suffers longer.

🔴 Red Light

  • Take on conservative options — play safe
  • Reset early
  • Don't rush decisions or execution
  • Don't hog the disc
  • When playing D — play tight, no poaching, no taking risks

If there is a turnover on a progressive option (reset and swing passes don't count), the thrower must go to jail for 10 seconds while play continues. The timekeeper can also send you to jail if you are being too risky (e.g. throwing a contested huck, poaching off on D).

🟢 Green Light

  • Take on progressive options
  • Try to throw break throws or hucks
  • Don't rush to reset the disc
  • Set yourself up in the active lanes
  • When playing D — take risks, look to poach into the lane
4:40

Game to 3 — Standard Ultimate

Full-field standard ultimate to close out the session.
